# PRODUCT.md — WheelyGood

register: product

## What it is

A sim racing wheel settings companion PWA. Three jobs: explain what every wheel/pedal/in-game setting does (Simpedia), map "my car does X" symptoms to ordered setting fixes (Troubleshooter), and store/share tuned setting profiles (Saves). Static app, all data baked in, offline after first load.

## Users

Nathan and his son Josh. Two people, known hardware (three fixed rigs: ClubSport DD on Xbox Series X, ClubSport V2.5 on Series S, Logitech G920 on PC), two games (Forza Horizon 6, F1 25). Used on the phone while sitting AT the rig, wheel in front of them, often mid-session between races. Also browsed on desktop when planning.

## Scene sentence (theme)

A racer sitting in a dim room lit by a TV, wheel rig in front of them, phone in hand, glancing between the app and the on-wheel OLED menu. Dark theme is forced by the scene, not the category.

## Tone

Confident garage-manual voice. Short, direct, technical but friendly ("My car does X — what do I change?"). Aussie-casual is fine. Never corporate, never gamified-cute.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard styling (cards everywhere, hero metrics)
- Sim-racing forum walls of text
- Gamer-RGB aesthetic (neon gradients, angular clip-paths, fake HUDs)

## Strategic principles

- Speed-to-answer beats completeness: the user is mid-session with a race waiting.
- The active setup + game context should do the filtering; the user should never re-state their rig.
- Data is hand-verified against official sources; unverified content is visibly flagged.
- Two-person app: personalisation over generality (their exact hardware is the whole hardware list).
